Bachelor Thesis Executive Summary

"Geography as a Determinant of Power: Analyzing the Impact of Germany's Geography on its Global Affairs (1900 — Present)"

Research Objective: This thesis evaluates the extent to which physical geography and strategic location in Central Europe dictated Germany's foreign policy doctrines, alliance formations, and military posture across major geopolitical epochs.

Key Core Analytical Pillars:

  • Mitteleuropa Positioning: Strategic vulnerabilities of the North German Plain.
  • Two-Front Dilemma: How encirclement anxieties shaped military doctrines from 1900 to 1945.
  • Cold War Division: The Rhine-Elbe frontier as the geopolitical fault line of NATO & Warsaw Pact.
  • Post-Unification Foreign Policy: Economic hegemony and multilateral diplomacy in the European Union.

Academic Result: Awarded highest marks, contributing directly to the Gold Medal standing across the graduating class of 80 students.
